## 1. Decision identity

**Question:** May the existing four-realm settlement/landmark/castle sheets be
cited as directional silhouette language for Stonehold capital, village,
dwelling, ruin, well, and Accordant-event architecture, without treating them
as per-family world-space production sheets?

**Already approved:**

- Four-realm landmark anchor, settlement kit, castle layout, and modular construction master (FourRealmArchitecture.md, 2026-07-24).
- Stonehold grammar: broad, low, compressed masses; stepped basalt foundations; thick buttresses; clipped-corner openings; soot-aged iron, dark timber, heavy masonry, bronze repairs, localized forge amber.
- Identity must survive without smoke, sparks, banners, or glowing cracks.

**Still undecided:**

- Per-family world-space kits for capital, village, dwelling, ruin, well/cistern.
- Accordant Isle is event-only cherry-blossom / sealed approaches (memory + topology); this packet does not redesign it.
- New ComfyUI Local per-family settlement sheets (none generated in this lane).

solid decorative blocks. Ruin, dwelling, and well variants (intact/worn/broken)

lowest LOD; drop banners and smoke first.
