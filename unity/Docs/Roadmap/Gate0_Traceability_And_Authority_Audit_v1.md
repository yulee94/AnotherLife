## 1. Stop-ship findings

### SS-20260828-001-A — missing governed identities and baseline records

The package defines mandatory `GOV-G0-v*`, `RC-*`, and `RB-*` identities, but the
three audited artifacts declare none. No canonical
`Candidates/<candidate-id>/change-set.md` or
`Baselines/<baseline-id>/manifest.md` exists. Therefore the audit cannot bind the
integrated DAG, evidence, diff, and parent baseline to one immutable candidate.
This violates the governance control at lines 46–55, the canonical registry at
lines 67–76, and `GT-00` at line 228. Missing candidate identity and baseline
provenance are `FAIL-CLOSED`.

### SS-20260828-001-B — unauthorized and contradictory approval status

`Gate0_Immutable_Authority_Register_v1.md:7` claims `immutable, owner-approved
planning baseline`, while `Gate0_Evidence_Governance_And_Stage_Gates_v1.md:7` and
`Gate0_Integrated_Delivery_DAG_v1.md:7` identify candidates and the final
approval/lock task `t_a4c586ff` remains `todo`. Individual source authorities are
owner-approved, but that does not authorize this newly assembled artifact to claim
approved-baseline status. The package must remain a candidate until the exact final
package receives explicit owner approval and an append-only baseline/approval record.

### Required remediation and reopen

1. Assign the governance control, roadmap candidate, and parent baseline their
   required immutable identities; add the canonical candidate change set and retained
   baseline manifest.
2. Make all three artifact statuses consistent and candidate-only. Do not mark the
   register or roadmap approved before the explicit `t_a4c586ff` owner decision.
3. Re-run this audit against the corrected exact candidate. A changed candidate or
   new baseline identity invalidates this disposition and requires fresh reference,
   edge, coverage, and prohibited-invention validation.
4. Preserve the previously approved board roadmap and do not promote, dispatch, or
   expose any scope on the authority of this unapproved package.
