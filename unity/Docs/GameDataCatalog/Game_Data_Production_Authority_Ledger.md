## Ownership terms

“Responsible maintainer” identifies who must assemble and review the proposed source in the repository; it does not transfer a user-owned decision. Under [`Ownership_Decision_Record.md`](../Ownership_Decision_Record.md):

- **Codex coordination/review mode (A1)** owns sequencing, authority disposition, provenance review, and integration readiness.
- **Codex narrative/content mode** owns player-facing names, localization references, and content meaning.
- **Codex engineering mode** owns technical catalogs, balance-record encoding, validators, assets/import, runtime, and tests.
- **Authorized co-developer in the declared mode** may perform those responsibilities, while A1 retains sequencing and integration disposition.
- **User** retains final product, creative, visual-design, balance, activation, integrated-playtest, milestone, and release approval.

A maintainer may mark an input technically ready for user review. A maintainer may not mark a user-owned decision approved.

## Status vocabulary

- `unresolved — source missing`: no complete candidate source exists.
- `unresolved — partial evidence only`: observed/prototype evidence exists but is incomplete or non-authoritative.
- `unresolved — partial/disputed evidence`: candidate sources exist but conflict or remain unaccepted.
- `unresolved — source-resolved, approval/encoding pending`: a migration source is identified, but production approval and/or bounded encoding is absent.
- `unresolved — draft/unaccepted`: a proposed source exists only in an open or draft change.
- `unresolved — user balance decision missing`: technical evidence exists, but explicit owner balance acceptance is absent.
- `unresolved — complete source set absent, approval not requestable`: the separate final owner gate cannot be requested while any family input remains unresolved.
- `approved`: prohibited in this ledger. A later ledger version requires a complete immutable source contract, maintainer acceptance, and explicit owner evidence for every user-owned decision bound to the exact source-set fingerprint. Delegated bounded recommendations are not production approval.

Dependency qualifiers such as `; depends on skills` may be appended to an unresolved status without changing its base meaning.

Every item below is intentionally unresolved.

## Fail-closed production-generation rule

A production generator **must refuse before writing any output** unless all of the following are true for the exact candidate being generated:

1. the candidate contains exactly the required family set and no required family is missing;
2. every required family field maps to a ledger decision item and all 25 literal v003 family blocker IDs are absent from the candidate blocker list;
3. every item has one complete, immutable, versioned authoritative source whose revision/path/hash validates;
4. every item has a durable responsible-maintainer acceptance linked to that exact source version;
5. every item’s required user decision is explicitly recorded where the table requires one;
6. no source, value, identity, reference, provenance claim, maintainer disposition, or user decision is missing, disputed, stale, draft-only, ambiguous, or superseded;
7. the separate `approval.user_creative_balance` gate explicitly approves the exact complete source-set version/hash under review;
8. strict schema, identity, alias, range, cross-reference, provenance, canonical-byte, and deterministic-generation validation succeeds.

If any condition is false or cannot be proven, the only valid result is a typed ineligible/refused outcome with ordered diagnostics, `productionEligible: false`, no manifest/artifact publication, no partial output, no replacement of the last accepted snapshot, and no silent fallback to legacy/runtime defaults. A green validator, merged maintainer PR, draft source, historical observation, issue assignment, or absence of objection is never approval.

## Completion checklist for later decision work

For each of the 25 tracked items, the resolving change must append durable evidence for:

- exact authoritative source ID, repository path, revision, and raw SHA-256;
- responsible maintainer and explicit acceptance statement/date;
- exact user decision requested and explicit approve/reject evidence/date;
- rejected alternatives and why they are non-authoritative;
- validator/test evidence;
- final status, which remains unresolved until every required field above is present.

The global approval record must then identify the exact complete source-set version/hash reviewed. Until that happens, issue #183 remains blocked and production generation remains ineligible.
