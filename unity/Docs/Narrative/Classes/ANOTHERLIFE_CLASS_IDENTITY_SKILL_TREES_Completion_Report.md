## Completion statement

The source packet resolves the repository's three conflicting class-like rosters into one explicit playable canon:

- four class families;
- sixteen playable classes, four per family;
- all classes available to all four realms;
- one primary identity resource per class;
- three skill-tree branches per class;
- defining level 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 milestone skills;
- one recoverable level-50 mastery trial per class;
- one class-specific True Warmaster title, ten-piece set identity, unique relic, and RvR skill per class.

This is source completion for the general class identity layer. It does not provide every non-milestone node, numerical balance, runtime catalogs, save fields, services, UI, art assets, or production activation.

| Ranger | Sharpshooter, Stalker, Beastmaster, Druid |

The four later enum additions now have explicit homes:

- Paladin extends Warrior into protection, cleansing, secondary healing, and party support.
- Necromancer is a ritual Mage who studies death as part of the celestial life cycle.
- Druid gives Ranger its primary sustained healer and Worldroot support identity.
- Slayer is an Assassin-family anti-elite specialist built around threat reading, interruption, and earned execution windows.

Family is derived from the selected class. It is never independently selected or persisted. Class choice belongs to each character, so same-realm subcharacters may use different classes; ordinary skill respec does not change class.

## Legacy-name disposition

- Guardian remains the exact Guardian class.
- Barbarian becomes a Berserker legacy alias.
- Marksman becomes a Sharpshooter legacy alias.
- Shadow Assassin becomes a Shadowblade legacy alias.
- Nightmare remains a Nightstalker visual alias.
- Swordsman, Enchanter, Warlock, Hunter, Healer, and Forest Ranger remain visual or role references rather than extra classes.
- `Cursor` is retired as a playable-class label and is not silently renamed to `Curser`; its curse/debuff motifs are distributed among Necromancer, Infiltrator, and Nightstalker.
- The nine Forge identities remain freely editable appearance presets. Even the same-name Vanguard preset does not select or authorize the Vanguard class.
- `realm_strike`, `renewing_guard`, `warzone_burst`, and `warmaster_breaker` remain prototype skills and do not belong to any canonical tree.
- `prototype_true_warmaster` and its generic pieces remain migration and historical evidence only.

## Realm and presentation boundary

Every class can be played in every realm. Realm selection changes cultural framing, materials, heraldry, language, animation flavor, and VFX palette without changing class behavior or progression:

- Stonehold: impact rings, stone, metal, forge sparks.
- Eldergrove: leaves, roots, light ribbons, healing growth.
- Crownlands: crests, banners, clean arcs, heroic rays.
- Umbral: smoke blades, cracks, sigils, ash.

Class identity must remain readable through posture, equipment silhouette, major shapes, and animation, not color alone. Companions, servants, anchors, roots, and decoys remain bounded for performance and readability. Druid launch identity uses spectral Wild Aspects instead of requiring a full separate transformed-model roster.
