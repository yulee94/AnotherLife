## Habitat Passes

- widely spaced trunk masses and open understory survive grayscale;
- hollow bases, buttress ramps, and wet root channels remain separable;
- foreground, midground, and background remain readable without particles;
- dark loam lanes and root-edge contrast preserve navigation appearance;
- vegetation remains sparse enough to avoid carpet or transparency dependence;
- the low/mobile study removes detail while retaining the habitat silhouette;
- Moonroot-directed wet-channel transition is visually coherent.

The passes above concern broad visual intent. Dense foreground roots, hanging
growth, and dark value compression locally weaken the required open
understory; production must reduce that clutter rather than treating the
generated density as authoritative.

## Placement Passes

- Grove Striders remain subordinate to habitat scale;
- long neck, narrow leg space, leaf-like ears, and bark/lichen hierarchy read;
- the layout sheet includes a neutral Champion comparison;
- no rider, saddle, reins, faction ornament, combat pose, or spawn UI appears;
- reduced views retain a tall-browser presence.

These placement passes do not validate the pictured proportions. The canonical
sheet and normalized written values remain authoritative wherever a generated
specimen reads shorter, smaller, more armored, crown-like, or deer-like.

## Concerns

- multiple root spans and hollow cavities can read as gates, portals, or doors;
- the Sunmane transition is inferred from a dry shelf, not explicitly pictured;
- several generated Grove Striders drift from canonical proportions and surface
  restraint; none replace the immutable creature sheet;
- some generated ear fins drift toward radial crowns/antlers and dorsal ridges
  toward sharp armor; both interpretations are rejected;
- one scale panel shows differently sized Striders without proving age,
  juvenile state, or variant authority;
- the pictured Champion comparison does not prove the normalized `1.8`
  head-height target and must not override it;
- exact trunk, root, cavity, slope, shelf, and clearance dimensions are absent;
- layout evidence is perspective-based rather than measured orthographic;
- kit pivots, scale ranges, density, and repetition thresholds are absent;
- effects-off evidence is visual, not a runtime capture;
- material swatches do not prove PBR ranges, texel density, alpha policy, or
  shader assumptions;
- the lowest illustrative reduction lets smaller Strider silhouettes and route
  boundaries merge into one dark root field;
- camera pixel coverage and supported recognition distance are unmeasured.
- normalized Strider `7–9` clip / `5–7 MiB` targets conflict with the parent
  generic low/mobile `5–6` clip / `3–4 MiB` lane and `6 MiB` maximum; this
  source packet does not resolve the coordination-level production budget.

## Optimization And Measurement

Measured in this source packet:

- three new PNGs and zero duplicated/retained input rasters;
- exact bytes, hashes, dimensions, opacity, and LFS identity;
- source-wave and decoded-review ceilings;
- one canonical existing Grove Strider reference by exact hash and GUID;
- zero Player/runtime placement.

Not measured because production assets/runtime are absent:

- triangles, terrain vertices, collider complexity, draw calls, overdraw;
- texture residency, mesh memory, animation memory, streaming, occlusion, or
  instancing;
- transparency cost, foliage card density, shadow cost, or shader variants;
- frame time, install/download delta, or representative device performance;
- exact sight range, camera coverage, or `96 px`, `64 px`, and `32 px`
  recognition thresholds.

These remain coordination/engineering acceptance gates after user approval.
Merging is not user visual approval, technical handoff, production approval,
integrated acceptance, milestone completion, or release completion.
