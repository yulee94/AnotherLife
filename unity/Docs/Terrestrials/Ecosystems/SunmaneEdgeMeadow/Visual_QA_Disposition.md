# Visual QA Disposition

## Disposition

- Overall: `PassWithConcern`
- Habitat: `ReadyForUserReview`
- Thornburrow Hare: `ReadyForUserReview`
- User approval: `NotRequested`
- Production integration: `Blocked`

Native-resolution inspection accepts the five sheets as coherent concept
evidence. They establish a serious open meadow, ordered edge language, a
recognizable adult Hare, action intent, grayscale checks, and scalable-quality
intent without relying on emission or particles.

## Accepted Evidence

- rolling terrain, shallow drainage, sightline pockets, broken tree line, and
  distant old-growth mass;
- distinct Hollowbark and Mirrorroot directional seams;
- eight documented kit semantics and subdued natural material language;
- low-head/hind-leg-wedge Hare with paired scraper tusks and blade tail;
- freeze, scrape, compression, two-stage bound, landing, and recovery intent;
- grayscale, static/reduced-motion, and distant-reduction illustrations.

## Concerns And Blocks

- The habitat paintings drift dry and brown; production must recover subdued
  green-gold meadow character without becoming lush or floral.
- The distant old-growth mass can read as an unsupported solitary giant-tree
  landmark. No landmark ID or gameplay meaning is authorized.
- The Mirrorroot strip overstates a broad water plane. Water begins outside
  the core kit and cannot dominate the Sunmane side.
- Both transitions, paths, slopes, scale, and review distances are illustrative
  and unmeasured.
- The Hare retains familiar rodent/capybara cues. Production must preserve the
  long rear-foot wedge, paired lower-jaw tusks, and blade tail as structural
  anatomy rather than accessory detail.
- Perspective makes pictured adults appear differently sized. This is not
  life-stage or population evidence.
- Tusks, rear feet, contact, and tail identity degrade at distance. Motion and
  LOD panels are paintovers, not topology, deformation, or rig proof.
- The kit sheet shows size variations, not a verifiable count of production
  meshes. The specification—not the painting—defines exactly eight families.
- Exposed-root studies can read piled or constructed and require a naturally
  embedded correction.
- No wet-edge Hare variant is pictured.
- No runtime memory, frame-time, build-size, install-size, device, streaming,
  camera, collision, navigation, water, animation, or accessibility test exists.

These concerns block production approval but do not prevent user review of the
exact source. Required next evidence is a measured adult turnaround and scale
blockout, anatomy/contact and deformation studies, verified LOD silhouettes,
measured habitat plan/sections, both transition lengths, effects-off captures,
and low-tier static/reduced-motion tests.
