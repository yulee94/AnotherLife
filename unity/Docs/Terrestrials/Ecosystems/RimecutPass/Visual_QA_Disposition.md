# Rimecut Pass Visual QA Disposition

## Disposition

- Overall: `PassWithConcern`
- Habitat: `ReadyForUserReview`
- Rimefan placement: `ReadyForUserReview`
- Rimefan immutable identity source: unchanged `PassWithConcern`
- User approval: `NotRequested`
- Production integration: `Blocked`

Native-resolution review accepts the three selected sheets as coherent concept
evidence for a cold slate-and-ice pass whose notch and broad ledges remain
visible without whiteout, glow, or particles.

## Accepted Evidence

- one strong pass notch, low snow saddles, eroded stone ribs, broad ledges, and
  separated avalanche chutes;
- natural Faultroad thinning and Ore Gallery melt-channel/cave-throat seams;
- restrained non-emissive materials and sparse lee-pocket organics;
- grayscale, black-shape, weather-off, and progressive-density studies;
- representative Rimefan perch, brace, and soar placement consuming the
  established adult identity.

## Concerns And Production Blocks

- Several safe ledge edges form long straight shelves that can read as a built
  retaining wall or road. Production must break them through irregular bedding,
  talus interruption, and erosion without losing the safe-edge value read.
- The concept sheets remain illustrative: route continuity, ledge width, slope,
  chute separation, pass distance, and transition lengths are unmeasured.
- Some background fins remain more dramatic than the intended compressed
  horizon. Needle-peak or ice-palace escalation is prohibited.
- The kit sheet visually isolates more pieces than the eight semantic families
  because material swatches and variations appear beside hero forms. Only the
  documented eight IDs are authoritative.
- The ice-shelf study includes heavy hanging ice forms. These cannot become the
  default family silhouette or a crystal-cave identity.
- Ore Gallery cave mouths are only qualitative and may be confused with small
  dark cavities. Measured seam evidence must preserve a few broad throats.
- Perched distant Kite views do not prove its diamond wing or compact wedge
  tail. Flight views drift toward familiar raptor wing/tail proportions.
- The generated Kite cannot supersede the established original-skull,
  five-terminal-group, tail, scale, topology, deformation, or normal-speed
  animation concerns in `AvianSoarers/Visual_QA_Disposition.md`.
- Same-adult repetitions are alternative studies, not flock or population
  evidence.
- No effects-off runtime capture, collision, navigation, avalanche, weather,
  footprint, memory, frame-time, build-size, install-size, streaming, camera,
  device, or accessibility test exists.

Rejected drafts are not retained: the first implied a flock, used arrows, and
created needle peaks; a second used portrait framing and confused the pass with
the chute. Production needs measured plan/sections, effects-off captures,
irregular natural ledge correction, verified transition strips, and exact
Rimefan distance silhouettes after user approval.
