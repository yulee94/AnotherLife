  It has no stable landmark or gameplay ID.

  three-part shovel forefeet, and flattened tail—not palette.

- Foreclaw digit/root continuity varies, and the tail sometimes becomes long

- Motion is illustrative, not normal-speed weight, deformation, contact, or
  audio proof. The whistle has no audio authority.
- Same-adult repetitions are alternative studies, not pack, sex, age, or
  population evidence. Both structural variants remain unpictured.
- The retained motion input used airborne spoil/vapor and the retained kit
