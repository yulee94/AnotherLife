## Habitat Passes

- low water horizon and distant tree line establish littoral scale;
- irregular shoreline, pale shallow stones, and darker deep water separate;
- flooded root shelves and mud fans retain natural rather than constructed read;
- sparse reeds and leaves preserve open shoreline sight gaps;
- deeper and rising/drier directions are suggested, but neither named neighbor
  transition is complete or independently recognizable;
- grayscale and simplified panels retain broad shoreline and depth hierarchy;
- the low/mobile intent is stated, but the creature proxy does not yet preserve
  feet/contact before cyan-ring detail.

The passes concern broad visual intent only. Reflections, cloud value, and
cyan creature markings still contribute materially to the pictured read;
production must prove equivalent recognition with those layers disabled. No
view fully disables reflection, ripple, flicker, and specular response
together.

## Placement Passes

- Lumenbacks remain subordinate to habitat scale in the establishing view;
- low dome, throat pouch, paddle feet, short tail, and mouth-rooted feelers
  remain recognizable at close and medium placement;
- shallow-water contact is physically plausible as a still image;
- no rider, saddle, armor strap, combat pose, UI, or explicit spawn marker appears;
- effects-reduced views retain a compact amphibious presence.

These passes do not validate scale, age, population, swimming, buoyancy,
emission, or motion. The canonical sheet and normalized written values remain
authoritative wherever a generated specimen differs.

## Concerns

- pale cyan rings remain high-contrast and can read emissive despite no light spill;
- ring count, arrangement, and brightness drift across generated specimens;
- distant specimens lose pouch, paddle-foot, feeler, and ring readability;
- differently sized/perspectively scaled animals do not prove juveniles,
  sex, life stages, variants, herd size, or population; all are the same
  standard adult shown in perspective;
- the broad domed back can drift toward a shell read if production segmentation
  or material breakup becomes too regular;
- dense root bundles can drift toward constructed fascines, revetments, or
  shoreline engineering instead of naturally flooded roots;
- the lowest creature proxy retains cyan rings after feet and stable contact
  weaken, which violates the required reduction hierarchy;
- exact shoulder/length scale, water depth, shore slope, root shelf, mud fan,
  and safe-contact dimensions are absent;
- the spatial evidence is perspective-based rather than measured orthographic;
- transition destinations are visual suggestions, not labeled boundary studies;
- kit pivots, scale ranges, density, atlas, and repetition thresholds are absent;
- static/effects-off evidence is painted, not a runtime capture;
- no fully reflection/ripple/flicker/specular-disabled water comparison exists;
- no planar reflection is required or authorized by these paintings;
- swatches do not prove PBR values, texel density, alpha policy, water shader,
  refraction, reflection, or depth implementation;
- camera pixel coverage and `96 px`, `64 px`, and `32 px` recognition distances
  are unmeasured;
- specialized Lumenback `7–9` clips / `3–5 MiB` conflict with the generic
  low-lane `5–6` clips / `3–4 MiB` target; this packet does not resolve it.

## Optimization And Measurement

Measured in this source packet:

- three new PNGs and zero duplicated/retained input rasters;
- exact bytes, hashes, dimensions, opacity, and LFS identity;
- source-wave and decoded-review ceilings;
- one canonical existing Lumenback reference by exact hash, LFS identity, and GUID;
- zero Player/runtime placement.

Not measured because production assets/runtime are absent:

- triangles, terrain/water vertices, collider complexity, draw calls, overdraw;
- texture/water residency, mesh/animation memory, streaming, occlusion, or
  instancing;
- transparency, reflection, refraction, reed card, shadow, or shader-variant cost;
- frame time, install/download delta, thermal, battery, or device performance;
- exact sight range, camera coverage, depth safety, or recognition thresholds.

These remain coordination/engineering acceptance gates after user approval.
Merging is not user visual approval, technical handoff, production approval,
integrated acceptance, milestone completion, or release completion.
