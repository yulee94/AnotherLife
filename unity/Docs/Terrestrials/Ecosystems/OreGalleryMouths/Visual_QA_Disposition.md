# Ore Gallery Mouths Visual QA Disposition

## Disposition

- Overall: `PassWithConcern`
- Habitat: `ReadyForUserReview`
- Standard adult Oreveil: `ReadyForUserReview`
- User approval: `NotRequested`
- Production integration: `Blocked`

The five selected sheets establish a coherent non-emissive cave-mouth habitat,
same-adult Oreveil material identity, motion intent, two seams, eight kit
semantics, and effects-independent value studies. They are concept evidence,
not measured anatomy, terrain, animation, or runtime proof.

## Concerns And Blocks

- Even after rejecting a clearly repeated façade draft, some selected mouths
  retain arch-like repetition and some columns read as designed buttresses.
  Production must increase unequal collapse, bedding continuity, talus
  interruption, and silhouette asymmetry.
- The cave-throat kit hero can read as a tent or constructed portal. It is a
  semantic placeholder only.
- Safe ground, throat size, column loading, chamber depth, seam length, and
  slopes are illustrative and unmeasured.
- Deep openings approach black crush and wet ground can rely too much on
  specular separation. Effects-off measured captures remain required.
- The written anatomy lock is not consistently proven by generated pixels.
  Open views vary around nine trunk plates and the underside visibly overcounts
  or ambiguously groups the required seven locomotor pairs.
- The broad sensory shield, two mouth appendages, and tail latch need measured
  orthographic and underside studies.
- Generated curls remain too circular and generic. Production must create the
  specified asymmetric oval shield-to-tail latch and prohibit rolling.
- The source remains pill-bug adjacent. Originality must come from the broad
  sensory shield, lateral keel rhythm, recessed head, tail latch, and grouped
  ripple motion—not palette.
- Distant kit placement grows tall exposed legs and loses the low tucked
  underside fringe. It cannot be used as an LOD mesh reference.
- Same-adult repetitions are alternative studies, not colony, sex, age, or
  population evidence. The deeper-gallery structural variant is unpictured.
- No measured scale, topology, deformation, normal-speed motion, effects-off
  runtime capture, collision, navigation, memory, frame-time, build/install
  size, streaming, camera, device, or accessibility evidence exists.

Rejected drafts are not retained: the first identity had inconsistent counts
and a perfect ball curl; the first habitat had three equal façade openings.
Required production follow-up is a measured nine-plate/seven-pair turnaround,
shield/mouth/tail attachments, oval curl deformation, verified low-tier
silhouette, irregular cave-throat plan/sections, and both measured seams.
