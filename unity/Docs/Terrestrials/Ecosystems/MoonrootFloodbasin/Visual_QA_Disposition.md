## Habitat Review

Passes:

- low water plane and high root canopy;
- asymmetric island/channel composition;
- root openings and depth values without glow;
- Hollowbark and Mirrorroot transition direction;
- reduced reflection and grayscale presentation;
- kit reuse and zero-required-particle intent.

Concerns:

- repeated shelf edges can resemble paving;
- the split buttress can resemble a designed gate;
- several elevated panels are illustrative rather than measured orthographic
  plan/section evidence;
- exact navigation, water depth, terrain density, and cell population remain
  outside A2 authority.

The habitat is reviewable, but production environment work remains blocked.

## Fauna Review

Passes:

- corrected `0.35` body/`0.70` wingspan intent;
- heavy thorax and pitched opaque roof;
- six-leg and four-wing spread anatomy;
- vertical rest and broad flood-season pads;
- non-emissive wet-bark/mica palette;
- climb/launch/land/fold motion grammar;
- black folded and spread silhouettes.

Concerns:

- profile occlusion prevents every far-side root from being independently
  counted in every panel;
- the rostrum must move farther from a bird-beak read;
- the coarsest generated proxy needs a clearer primary contact footprint;
- the farthest reduced habitat example loses presence entirely;
- exact pixel-coverage distance is unmeasured.

The flood-season ecotype is reviewable with those concerns. The dry-season
ecotype is not pictured and remains `ProposedTextOnly`.
